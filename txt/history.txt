1. The origins of this manuscript are unknown, but it has been carbon dated to have been made sometime between <date notBefore="1404" notAfter="1438">1404 and 1438</date>.
2. Any history between <date notBefore="1404" notAfter="1576">1404 and 1576</date> is missing and unknown.

5. Owned by Georgius Barschius potentially sometime between <date notBefore="1627" notAfter="1637">1627 and 1637</date>.
6. Johannes Marcus Marci of Kronland was gifted Barschius' alchemical library in <date when="1677">1677</date>, which included the Voynich Manuscript.

8. H. P. Kraus purchases the manuscript from Ethel Voynich, Wilfrid Voynich's widow.
9. Given to the Beinecke Library by H. P. Kraus
