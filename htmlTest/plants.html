Theodore Petersen

Fr. Petersen first made a full photocopy of the Voynich manuscript’s photostats, then created a handwritten copy of the manuscript, consulting the original in a New York safe deposit vault when passages were especially difficult. He finished this work in 1944, adding notes on possible meanings, marking unusual character sequences, highlighting common words, and suggesting plant identifications in the herbal section based in part on Holm and O’Neill’s research. He also compiled a complete concordance of the manuscript’s words, though the writer had not seen it. Petersen remained a leading Voynich expert until his death, after which his papers were passed to Friedman, inventoried by Tiltman, and eventually housed at the George C. Marshall Library and Archives in Lexington, Virginia.
